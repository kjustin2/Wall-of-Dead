# Wall of Dead — Polish Ideas

A menu of polish ideas for the vertical slice. Pick whatever you like and I'll
build them. Effort is rough: **S** ≈ quick, **M** ≈ a session, **L** ≈ bigger.
Nothing here changes the core (behind-the-wall night defense → day supply run →
victory); it all makes the existing slice deeper and more satisfying.

Tip: just tick the boxes you want (or list the numbers) and hand it back.

---

## 1. Game feel & juice

- [ ] **1.1 Hit-stop on big hits** (S) — freeze the frame 30–60ms on brute hits,
      headshots, and grenade kills. Cheap, huge impact on weight.
- [ ] **1.2 Muzzle smoke + heat haze** (S) — lingering smoke puff at the muzzle;
      a subtle shimmer when firing fast.
- [ ] **1.3 Shell casings** (S) — eject casings that bounce off the rampart.
- [ ] **1.4 Blood decals on the ground** (M) — pooled splats that fade, so the
      kill zone in front of the wall reads as a battlefield.
- [ ] **1.5 Ragdoll-ish death variety** (M) — a few death animations (topple
      forward, blown back, crumple) instead of one sink.
- [ ] **1.6 Dismemberment on headshots/explosions** (M) — pop the head/limb off
      as a flying chunk. Very satisfying for a zombie game.
- [ ] **1.7 Kill streak feedback** (S) — escalating "x3 / x5 / RAMPAGE" call-outs
      and a rising audio pitch as you chain kills.
- [ ] **1.8 Reload feel** (S) — a small on-gun reload animation + a click-clack,
      and a brief "RELOADING" radial timer near the crosshair.
- [ ] **1.9 Low-ammo tension** (S) — the ammo counter pulses red at ≤25%, a dry-
      click when empty, a distinct "last bullet" ping.
- [ ] **1.10 Damage direction indicator** (S) — red arc on the screen edge toward
      the zombie that just hit you / the breaching segment.
- [ ] **1.11 Slow-mo on the final kill of the night** (S) — brief time dilation +
      push-in as dawn breaks.
- [ ] **1.12 Crosshair that reacts** (S) — bloom/spread the reticle while firing,
      tighten when still; turn red over a valid target.

## 2. Combat depth

- [ ] **2.1 Real headshot skill** (M) — let vertical aim matter (raise/lower the
      aim plane) so headshots are earned, not a flat 20% roll.
- [ ] **2.2 Weak points** (M) — spitter sac and brute head as destructible weak
      points with bonus damage.
- [ ] **2.3 Penetration feedback** (S) — rifle pierce shows a through-and-through
      effect and a combo tick per pierced zombie.
- [ ] **2.4 Melee shove / kick** (M) — a short-cooldown shove that knocks a
      zombie off the wall when they're clawing right in front of you.
- [ ] **2.5 Overheat / recoil climb** (S) — sustained SMG fire climbs recoil,
      rewarding bursts.
- [ ] **2.6 Critical hit system** (S) — small crit chance with a distinct
      gold number + sound, scaling with Adrenaline.

## 3. Enemies & AI

- [ ] **3.1 One more zombie type** (M) — e.g. a **Screamer** that buffs/aggros
      nearby zombies, or a **Crawler** that's low and hard to hit, or an
      **Armored** shambler that needs sustained fire.
- [ ] **3.2 Mini-boss at the dawn surge** (L) — a hulking **Tank** that soaks the
      final wave and telegraphs a wall-smashing charge.
- [ ] **3.3 Smarter pathing to breaches** (M) — zombies actively funnel toward
      the weakest/broken segment instead of their spawn lane.
- [ ] **3.4 Climbers** (M) — some zombies climb the barricade (animated) rather
      than only clawing, raising the stakes on a held segment.
- [ ] **3.5 Spawn tells** (S) — distant silhouettes + groans before a wave so the
      field feels alive and you can pre-aim.
- [ ] **3.6 Idle/ambient horde** (S) — far-off shapes shambling on the horizon
      that never reach you, for dread.

## 4. The wall / defense

- [ ] **4.1 Mid-night repair** (M) — hammer a segment during a lull (hold E at a
      breach) trading exposure for integrity.
- [ ] **4.2 Barricade upgrades** (M) — spend day supplies on spikes (damage
      attackers), a higher segment, or a repaired post.
- [ ] **4.3 Deployables** (M) — place a handful of bear traps / barbed wire /
      a turret on the field during setup.
- [ ] **4.4 Segment health pips** (S) — small per-segment damage indicators on
      the wall itself so you can read where it's failing at a glance.
- [ ] **4.5 Breach drama** (S) — alarm sting, a red floodlight on the broken
      segment, and a "BREACH!" call-out.

## 5. Adrenaline & abilities

- [ ] **5.1 Grenade cook + arc preview** (M) — hold F to charge throw distance
      with a dotted landing arc + target ring; release to throw.
- [ ] **5.2 Multiple throwables** (M) — choose Frag / Molotov (lingering fire
      zone) / Flashbang (stun) for the Last Stand spend.
- [ ] **5.3 Adrenaline visual ramp** (S) — desaturate + heartbeat at low meter,
      warm bloom + faint slow-mo at Surge, so the zone is felt not just shown.
- [ ] **5.4 "Adrenaline overdrive" alt-spend** (M) — instead of a grenade, spend
      the meter for a few seconds of infinite ammo + fire rate.
- [ ] **5.5 Partial charges** (S) — let the meter bank a charge so you can throw
      one frag without needing a full bar.

## 6. Weapons & loadout

- [ ] **6.1 A 5th weapon** (S) — e.g. a **crossbow** (silent, pierces) or a
      **flamethrower** (cone DPS, area denial).
- [ ] **6.2 Weapon pickups on the field** (M) — a dropped weapon crate that lands
      mid-night and you must expose yourself to grab.
- [ ] **6.3 Per-weapon muzzle/tracer identity** (S) — distinct flash color, tracer
      length, and recoil per gun so they feel different.
- [ ] **6.4 Attachments from supplies** (L) — extended mag, suppressor, scope as
      day rewards that tweak weapon stats.
- [ ] **6.5 Last-bullet auto-reload polish** (S) — smarter reload timing + a
      "tactical vs empty" reload distinction.

## 7. Day minigame (Supply Run)

- [ ] **7.1 Compass arrow to nearest crate** (S) — an on-screen chevron pointing
      to the closest uncollected crate when none are on screen.
- [ ] **7.2 Extraction phase** (M) — after grabbing, sprint back to a glowing
      extraction zone before the timer ends for a tier bonus.
- [ ] **7.3 Crate variety** (S) — rare gold crates (worth 2) and risky crates
      ringed by zombies for greed/risk decisions.
- [ ] **7.4 A second day activity** (L) — alternate between Supply Run and e.g.
      a **Rescue** (escort a survivor to safety = new companion) chosen as a card.
- [ ] **7.5 Day expedition choice screen** (M) — pick from 2–3 risk/reward runs
      (safe/low loot vs dangerous/high loot) before playing.
- [ ] **7.6 Stamina/sprint** (S) — a sprint button with a stamina bar to make the
      dash tactical.
- [ ] **7.7 Better day lighting** (S) — golden-hour sun angle, longer shadows,
      god-rays through the wreckage to sell "dawn".

## 8. Companions / allies

- [ ] **8.1 Companion banter** (S) — short barks ("On your left!", "Reloading!",
      "They're at the gate!") via floating text + voice-blip synth.
- [ ] **8.2 Down-but-revivable** (M) — a downed ally can be revived (hold E)
      within a window before they're lost.
- [ ] **8.3 Assign a companion to a segment** (M) — point them to hold a specific
      part of the wall.
- [ ] **8.4 Rescue → roster growth** (M) — the day Rescue activity adds a named
      companion for the next night (ties to 7.4).
- [ ] **8.5 Companion personalities** (S) — different ally types (marksman = slow
      but strong, gunner = fast but weak).

## 9. World & rendering

- [ ] **9.1 Volumetric light shafts** (M) — god-rays from the moon, searchlights,
      and the flashlight cone (fake via additive cones) for atmosphere.
- [ ] **9.2 Flashlight shadows** (M) — let the flashlight cast shadows so zombies
      throw long shadows toward you. Big mood win.
- [ ] **9.3 Rain / weather** (M) — a rain night with ripple particles, wet sheen,
      and heavier fog; storms tie into the lightning.
- [ ] **9.4 Reactive sky** (S) — the dawn ramp pushes color into clouds; the moon
      sets as the sun hints at the horizon.
- [ ] **9.5 Wreckage detail pass** (M) — burned-out tank/helicopter, sandbag
      variety, hanging cables, a flickering broken street lamp.
- [ ] **9.6 Ground texture/decals** (M) — cracks, scorch marks, painted road
      lines via procedural canvas textures (still asset-free).
- [ ] **9.7 Distant battle ambience** (S) — far-off muzzle flashes + booms on the
      horizon so you're not the only fight in the world.
- [ ] **9.8 Camera breathing + aim lean** (S) — subtle idle sway and a lean toward
      where you aim, for a handheld feel.
- [ ] **9.9 Color-grade per phase** (S) — cold blue deep night → teal late night →
      warm amber dawn, tuned as distinct looks.

## 10. Audio

- [ ] **10.1 Dynamic music layers** (M) — a synth score that adds layers with wave
      intensity and swells at the dawn surge.
- [ ] **10.2 Positional SFX** (M) — pan zombie groans / acid / breaches by their
      x-position so threats are audible off-screen.
- [ ] **10.3 Heartbeat at low HP** (S) — muffled audio + heartbeat when near death.
- [ ] **10.4 Tighter weapon audio** (S) — punchier layered shots, distinct
      tails per weapon, a satisfying reload foley.
- [ ] **10.5 Dawn payoff sting** (S) — a hopeful musical resolve + birdsong as the
      night is survived.
- [ ] **10.6 UI sound pass** (S) — hover/confirm/back sounds, meter-full chime,
      weapon-swap clicks.

## 11. UI / UX / HUD

- [ ] **11.1 Wave/threat readout** (S) — "Wave building", "SURGE INCOMING",
      "DAWN in 0:20" so the player can pace themselves.
- [ ] **11.2 Off-screen threat markers** (S) — edge arrows for crossing zombies
      coming around a breach toward you.
- [ ] **11.3 Hitmarkers** (S) — a crosshair tick on hit, a heavier one on kill,
      gold on headshot.
- [ ] **11.4 Damage numbers toggle + styling pass** (S) — crisper floaters,
      stacking, crit emphasis; option to turn off.
- [ ] **11.5 Compact ammo/segment HUD redesign** (M) — a cohesive diegetic-ish
      frame; per-segment wall map.
- [ ] **11.6 Pause = quick stats** (S) — show run stats and controls on pause.

## 12. Menus, meta & progression

- [ ] **12.1 Restore the multi-night run** (L) — lift `legsTotal` back to 4 with
      escalating night plans and day choices between legs.
- [ ] **12.2 Run summary screen** (S) — a richer victory/defeat board (accuracy,
      best streak, time, supplies, MVP companion).
- [ ] **12.3 Persistent best scores** (S) — localStorage high scores on the title.
- [ ] **12.4 Difficulty options** (S) — Story/Normal/Nightmare presets tuning HP,
      spawn rate, and wall strength.
- [ ] **12.5 Title screen scene polish** (M) — a more composed hero shot, animated
      logo, "press any key", version tag.
- [ ] **12.6 Meta unlocks** (L) — earn supplies across runs to unlock weapons /
      starting companions / cosmetics.

## 13. Accessibility & options

- [ ] **13.1 Remappable keys** (M) — rebind movement/fire/reload/grenade.
- [ ] **13.2 Colorblind-safe palette option** (S) — alt colors for ally/threat
      markers and meters.
- [ ] **13.3 Reduced-flashing / no-shake modes** (S) — already have shake; add a
      photosensitivity-safe toggle that tames lightning/aberration/flash.
- [ ] **13.4 Aim assist / aim slowdown** (M) — optional slight pull toward heads.
- [ ] **13.5 Text size + subtitle options** (S) — scale HUD/story text.
- [ ] **13.6 FOV / camera distance slider** (S).

## 14. Narrative & atmosphere

- [ ] **14.1 Inter-night story beats** (S) — short radio/log lines between nights
      that advance the convoy story.
- [ ] **14.2 Environmental storytelling** (M) — readable signs, graffiti, a
      survivor's note near the wreck.
- [ ] **14.3 Radio chatter** (S) — periodic crackly comms during the night for
      mood and objectives.
- [ ] **14.4 Ending variations** (M) — different victory epilogues based on wall
      integrity / companions alive / supplies.
- [ ] **14.5 Name your defender** (S) — a tiny input at the start for ownership.

## 15. Performance & tech

- [ ] **15.1 Instanced zombies** (L) — instance the common body parts to push the
      horde count way up.
- [ ] **15.2 Object pooling for zombies** (M) — reuse zombie meshes instead of
      build/dispose per spawn (less GC churn).
- [ ] **15.3 Quality auto-detect** (S) — pick a starting quality preset from a
      quick FPS probe on boot.
- [ ] **15.4 Frame pacing / fixed timestep for physics** (M) — smoother behavior
      under frame spikes.

## 16. Onboarding / tutorial

- [ ] **16.1 First-night prompts** (S) — contextual tips ("A/D to move", "R to
      reload", "Meter full — press F") that appear once.
- [ ] **16.2 Practice/sandbox toggle** (S) — a no-fail mode to try weapons.
- [ ] **16.3 Control hints on the day screen** (S) — clarify WASD/grab for the
      minigame the first time.
